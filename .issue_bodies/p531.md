## 交付物
两个独立的MOSFET噪声PSD函数: mos_thermal_psd() 和 mos_flicker_psd()。

## 实现路径
文件: float_spice/float_spice.c, bsim4_eval()之后(~line 527)

mos_thermal_psd(gm, temp):
  S_id = 4 * k_B * T * (2/3) * gm
  k_B = 1.380649e-23f
  例: gm=1e-3, T=300 → ~1.1e-23 A^2/Hz

mos_flicker_psd(ids, coxe, Leff, freq, noia, noib):
