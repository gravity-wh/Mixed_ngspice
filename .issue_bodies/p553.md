## 交付物
dvt2(体偏压SCE) + rsw/rdw(独立源漏电阻)接入bsim4_eval()。

## 实现路径
1. dvt2 (~line 240, Vth SCE段):
   dVth_sce *= (1.0f + pp->dvt2 * Vbs)
   效果: Vbs负→SCE增强→Vth更低

2. rsw/rdw (~line 460, Rds段):
   替换单rdsw:
   rs_s = rsw/(Weff+1e-30f); rd_d = rdw/(Weff+1e-30f)
   rs_rdsw = rdsw/(Weff*(1+prwg*Vgsteff+1e-30f))
   使用max(rs_rdsw, rs_s/rd_d)每端

## 测试标准
- dvt2: Vbs=0 vs Vbs=-0.5V: Vth变化10-30mV
- rsw/rdw: rsw=80 vs rsw=0: Ids差异可观测
- 回归: 01_nmos_dc gm/vth不变(PTM lib中dvt2=0, rsw/rdw值小)
- cvtss2sd <= 5
